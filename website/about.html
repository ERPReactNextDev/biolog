About us

Built for field teams, not for HR departments

Most attendance software assumes an office, a desk and a reliable connection. Philippine field teams have none of those three.

Who we are

Software that assumes the signal is bad

BIOLOG was not designed in a conference room. It came out of watching a sales team lose half a day of attendance records because the building they worked from had no signal, and the app simply refused to submit.

So we built the opposite of that assumption. Offline is not a degraded mode we tolerate — it is the normal path, and the system is designed to tell you honestly which records were captured live and which were captured later.

Our mission

Reliable attendance, even with limited internet

A field agent's shift should be recorded the moment it happens — not whenever the network next cooperates. Everything we build comes back to that.

Never lose a record

If an agent took the photo, the shift happened. The system stores it locally and finds a way to file it later.

Show your uncertainty

A pin with no accuracy figure is a guess dressed as data. We render the number rather than hiding it.

Be honest to admins

Flag the records that need a human rather than burying them. A clean-looking report built on unexamined data is not clean.

How it fits together

Three systems, one record

BIOLOG is the product of combining what each of these does well — and of being honest about what each one cannot do alone.

Biometrics

A fingerprint scanner on a wall in an office answers "are you here?" for one person at a time. It does not answer where, and it stops working the moment the employee is at a client instead.

BIOLOG keeps the identity check — face verification against the registered agent — and drops the hardware.

Identity

Proves who took the photo. No shared devices, no borrowed phones, no logins handed around.

GPS

A coordinate without an accuracy figure is a guess. Most apps take the first reading the phone's radio offers, which indoors is routinely a block away — and then present it as fact.

BIOLOG listens for the best fix within a window and tells you what it got, including when it is poor enough to doubt.

Place

Proves where, with the accuracy attached. Plus a manual override when the GPS is simply wrong.

Offline

Neither of the above works in a barangay with no signal, which is exactly where a field team spends its day. A cloud-only system fails in the field and works fine in the office, which is the worst ratio of useful hours to total hours.

BIOLOG treats the offline case as the expected case and syncs when it can, rather than pretending the network is there.

Continuity

Proves it happened even when the network could not be told about it.

Biometrics + GPS + Offline = BIOLOG
