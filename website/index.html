What you get

Six things that make attendance stick

Not a feature list for its own sake — each of these exists because something about field work makes the obvious approach fail.

Photo & Face Verification

Every clock-in and site visit takes a photo. With facial verification on, a borrowed phone no longer works — which is the whole point of taking one.

Precise GPS Tracking

BIOLOG listens for the best fix within a 15-second window instead of accepting the first one. You see the accuracy figure, not just a dot.

Works Offline

No signal? The log is queued on the phone with its photo and coordinates and syncs itself when the connection returns. Nothing is lost, nothing is backdated.

Smart Reports

Attendance, hours, late arrivals and overtime, filterable by team or individual and exportable to Excel. Computed from the work day your company actually runs, not midnight.

Site Visit Logs

Client visits are recorded separately from attendance, with the client, contact and remarks attached. One row per visit, with photo proof and a timestamp you can hand to a client.

Admin Dashboard

See who's in, who isn't and who claimed a location they shouldn't have. Review flags, manage roles and permissions, and audit every administrative action.
