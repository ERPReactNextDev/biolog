Questions

Common questions

Is the mobile app included in every plan?
Yes. There is no separate charge for the mobile app or for the admin dashboard — a plan that gave you a dashboard without the app your team uses would be selling the wrong half of the product.
How does offline mode actually work?
When the app cannot reach the server, the log is written to the phone's local database with its photo and coordinates, then synced automatically once a connection returns. The agent sees a pending count so they know it has not yet been filed, and a record filled in offline is flagged as needing review rather than passing silently.
Do agents need to be near the office to clock in?
That depends on how you configure it. You set the office boundary and its radius, and you decide whether being outside it blocks the clock-in or simply flags the record for review. Client-site boundaries work the same way, per client.
What happens to our data if we leave?
It is yours. Every table exports to a single JSON file alongside a familiar Excel export, so you can keep the records or move them elsewhere. Credential columns are deliberately excluded from the JSON dump, but attendance history, site visits and locations are all included.
Can we add or remove users mid-month?
Yes. Seat changes take effect immediately and are billed on the next invoice at a prorated rate. Upgrading does not require a reinstall — the mobile app picks up the new permissions on next sign-in.
Is facial verification required?
No, it is a per-user toggle rather than a plan-wide switch. It is useful where devices are shared, and pointless where every agent has their own phone. You can enable it for some users and not others in the same install.
Do you support multiple companies in one install?
Professional and Enterprise do. Each company is scoped to its own users and records, so a shared install cannot leak one tenant's data into another's reports.
How long does onboarding take?
Typically a few days: we import your users, configure your work day, grace period and office boundary, then train the supervisors. Agent onboarding is deliberately short — the app is four buttons deep from the home screen.

Not sure which plan fits?

Tell us your headcount and field setup. We'd rather size it properly than have you upgrade in three months.
